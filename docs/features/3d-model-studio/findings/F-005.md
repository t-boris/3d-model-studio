## Finding

The requirement identifies the target but places no enforceable boundary on the changes. An operation such as extrusion can affect adjacent topology. The specification leaves open what the user authorizes by selecting faces and who authorizes any broader change.

> The agent uses the selection as the target of the requested edit.

## Possible interpretations

- Changes beyond the selected region require a separate confirmation, including changes needed to keep neighboring geometry connected.
- The selection permits consequential edits to connected geometry when required by the requested operation.

## Resolution

Если вытягивание выбранной грани сдвинет общую вершину или перестроит соседнюю грань, приложение покажет затронутую область и запросит подтверждение до применения правки. — see DEC-009.
